# Local Business SEO

## Goal
Make a real local business easy to understand, trust and find for high-intent local searches.

## Core information
Establish:
- exact business name
- main service categories
- real service area
- primary city/region
- phone/contact method
- opening/response expectations when known
- business differentiators
- real proof

Keep business information consistent.

## Information architecture
Typical useful structure:
- Home
- Service pages
- Areas served / selected location pages
- About / trust
- Portfolio or case studies
- Reviews/testimonials when real
- FAQ
- Contact / quote

Do not create pages merely to increase page count.

## Service pages
Each important service deserves a page when search intent and buying intent are distinct.

A strong service page includes:
- service definition
- problems it solves
- who it is for
- process
- proof
- geographic relevance
- objections
- CTA

Avoid thin near-duplicate service pages.

## Location pages
Create only for genuine served areas with meaningful differences or sufficient unique content.

A good location page may include:
- services available there
- travel/service conditions
- local examples/projects
- testimonials from the area
- local context relevant to the service
- clear contact path

Never create 50 pages that only swap the city name.

## On-page SEO
For every target page:
- one clear intent
- descriptive title
- one H1
- natural local terms
- logical headings
- internal links
- useful meta description
- image alt text when informative

Write for customers first.

## Local trust
Useful real signals:
- years operating
- certifications
- insurance/qualifications
- portfolio
- before/after
- local reviews
- service guarantees
- team/owner identity
- real address when appropriate

Do not fabricate proximity or local offices.

## Google Business Profile alignment
Website and GBP should agree on:
- business name
- categories
- service areas
- phone
- URL
- hours where applicable

Support GBP with strong relevant service/location pages.

## Schema
Use accurate structured data where appropriate:
- LocalBusiness
- Organization
- Service
- BreadcrumbList
- FAQPage only for visible FAQs

Do not mark up fake reviews or invisible content.

## Internal linking
Link:
- home → core services
- services → relevant areas
- areas → relevant services
- case studies → relevant service/location
- blog/content → commercial pages when useful

## Conversion
SEO traffic must have a clear next step:
- quote
- call
- booking
- contact

Put proof and CTA close to high-intent content.

## Measurement
Track:
- form submits
- calls/click-to-call
- bookings
- qualified lead source
- organic landing pages

Rankings alone are not the business outcome.
