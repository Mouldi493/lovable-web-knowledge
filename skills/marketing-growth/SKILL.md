# Marketing and Growth

## Start with measurement
Define:
- primary conversion
- funnel stages
- critical events
- acquisition source tracking
- reporting cadence

Do not optimize a channel without knowing the business outcome.

## Paid ads
Before launching:
- define audience
- offer
- landing-page intent match
- budget
- target CPA/ROAS logic
- creative angles
- conversion tracking

Do not scale spend when the landing page or tracking is clearly broken.

## Ad creative
Build creative around:
- hook
- problem or desire
- mechanism/demo
- proof
- CTA

Create meaningfully different concepts, not just tiny headline variations.

For UGC-style creative, keep scripts natural and product-specific. Do not fabricate testimonials.

## Email
Lifecycle priorities:
- welcome
- activation/onboarding
- abandoned cart or incomplete action when relevant
- post-purchase
- review request
- retention/replenishment
- win-back

Each email should have one dominant objective.

## Launch
A launch plan should include:
- audience
- message
- offer
- assets
- channels
- timeline
- tracking
- follow-up
- post-launch learning

## Analytics
Track meaningful events with consistent names.
Verify events fire correctly before trusting dashboards.

## Attribution
Use attribution as a decision aid, not absolute truth.
Recognize channel reporting bias and cross-device limitations.

## Experimentation
Every test needs:
- hypothesis
- primary metric
- expected mechanism
- meaningful variant
- sufficient traffic
- stop rule

Fix obvious UX defects directly instead of testing them.

## Growth ethics
No deceptive scarcity, fake reviews, fake savings, dark patterns, hidden fees or misleading ad-to-page mismatches.
